# Miyaar Developers: Outreach and Sales Scripts

Copy, personalise and send. Anything in `[brackets]` must be replaced; **never send a message with a bracket still in it.**
Golden rule: **talk about their problem, not about us.** Keep first messages under 80 words.

## 2. Local "demo-first" outreach (Google Maps: Pakistan + UAE)

**How to pick targets.** Look for businesses with **4.2+ stars and 30+ reviews** (they're doing well and can pay) and a visible problem: no website, a broken or slow site, not mobile-friendly, or no WhatsApp/booking button.

**Process:**
1. Fill the tracker row and note the problem you spotted.
2. For the best 25–30%, make a 20-minute homepage mockup from the niche template, using their name, logo, photos and real reviews.
3. Screenshot the mockup.
4. Send the message below.

### 2a. WhatsApp / email with mockup (English)
> Hi [Name], I'm [Your name] from Miyaar Developers. I found [Business] on Google Maps. [4.7 stars from 120 reviews] is impressive!
>
> I couldn't find a website for you, so people searching "[service] in [area]" are landing on competitors. I made a quick homepage design for [Business] (screenshot attached). **No cost.**
>
> If you like it, we can make it live in 7 days. Should I send you the full preview link?

### 2b. Same, in Roman Urdu
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], main [Your name] hoon, Miyaar Developers se. Google Maps par [Business] dekha. [120 reviews aur 4.7 rating], masha'Allah bohat acha hai!
>
> Lekin aap ki website nahi mili, is liye jo log "[service] in [area]" search karte hain woh competitors ke paas chale jaate hain. Main ne [Business] ke liye ek homepage design banaya hai (screenshot attached), **bilkul free.**
>
> Pasand aaye to hum 7 din mein isay live kar sakte hain. Kya main full preview link bhej doon?

### 2c. Without mockup (for businesses that already have a site with problems)
> Hi [Name], I checked [Business]'s website on my phone. It took [8] seconds to load and there's no WhatsApp/call button on the first screen. Most visitors leave in under 3 seconds, so that's likely costing you calls.
>
> I recorded a 3-minute video with 3 quick fixes. Want me to send it?

### 2d. Cold-call script (local or UAE; also works for US business lines)
> **Opener:** "Hi, is this [Name]? This is [Your name] from Miyaar Developers. I'll be quick. I was looking at [Business] on Google and noticed [specific problem]. Do you have 30 seconds?"
>
> **Problem:** "Businesses like yours with [X reviews] usually lose calls because [no site / slow site / no booking button]. Is getting more [bookings / calls / orders] a priority for you right now?"
>
> **Offer:** "We actually made a free homepage design for [Business]. Can I WhatsApp it to you? If you like it, we can talk for 15 minutes about making it live."
>
> **Close the step:** "What's the best WhatsApp number? And would [day, time] or [day, time] be better for a quick call?"
>
> **If they say "not interested":** "No problem. Is it because you're happy with how leads come in now, or just bad timing?" (Note the answer, then put them in Nurture for 30 days.)

## 5. Upwork proposal

**Filters:**
- Payment verified
- $1k+ spent
- Hire rate 50%+
- Posted in the last hour (aim to be in the first 5 proposals)
- Client in US/UK/CA/AU/UAE
- Skip vague $5 jobs

> Hi [Client name if visible],
>
> [First line repeats their exact problem, e.g. "You need a fast, mobile-friendly site for your cleaning business that turns visitors into booked quotes."] I've done exactly this before: [link to most relevant demo or case study].
>
> How I'd approach it:
> 1. [Step tailored to the job]
> 2. [Step]
> 3. [Step], delivered in [X] days.
>
> One question so I can quote accurately: [smart question about their goal, e.g. "Do quotes come mostly by phone or form today?"]
>
> Happy to jump on a 10-minute call today.
> [Your name], Miyaar Developers

**Rules:** under 150 words, one relevant sample (not a list of 10), and always end with a question.

## 7. Discovery call script (20–30 min)

1. **Frame (1 min):** "Thanks for the time. I'd like to ask a few questions about your business, then show you what we'd do. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you. Sound good?"
2. **Situation:** "How do customers find you today? Roughly how many enquiries a month?"
3. **Problem:** "What's not working? What have you tried?"
4. **Impact:** "What's one extra customer worth to you? So if you got [X] more a month, that's [value]?"
5. **Goal:** "If we worked together, what would success look like in 90 days?"
6. **Budget:** "Projects like this usually range from [low] to [high]. Where would you be comfortable?"
7. **Decision:** "Besides you, who's involved in the decision? When do you want this live?"
8. **Next step (always):** "I'll send a one-page proposal with 3 options by [tomorrow, time]. Can we book 15 minutes on [day] to go through it and decide?"

**Write down the decision date in the tracker before the call ends.**

## 8. One-page proposal structure

1. **Their goal** (in their words, from the call)
2. **The problem** we found, and what it costs them
3. **The plan:** 3–5 bullet steps with the timeline
4. **3 options:**

   | Good | Better (recommended) | Best |
   |---|---|---|
   | Launch Site | Launch Site + Lead Engine | Launch Site + Lead Engine + Automation + 3 months Growth Retainer |

5. **Proof:** one case study and one testimonial
6. **Terms:** 50% to start, 50% before go-live; 2 revision rounds; valid for 7 days
7. **Next step:** "Reply 'Go' with your chosen option and we'll send the deposit invoice. Kickoff within 24 hours of payment."

### Proposal follow-ups

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| **Day 2** | "Hi [Name], any questions on the proposal? Happy to adjust the scope to fit your budget." |
| **Day 5** | "Quick check-in. We have a kickoff slot open on [date]. Shall I hold it for [Company]?" (only if true) |
| **Day 10** | "Should I keep this open, or close it for now? Either is totally fine." |

## 9. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive." | "Totally fair. Which part matters most to you? We can start with just [smaller package] and add the rest later." (Cut scope, not price.) |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What specifically do you want to think through? …Shall we decide on [day]?" |
| "Send me your portfolio." | "Sure, here are the 2 most similar projects: [links]. Can we look at them together for 10 minutes so I can explain the results?" |
| "We already have a developer." | "Great. We often work alongside one. Where we usually help is [ads / automation / speed]. Is follow-up on leads handled automatically today?" |
| "We tried a website before; it didn't bring business." | "That's common. A site alone doesn't bring leads. That's why we add Google Business Profile, WhatsApp and [ads/SEO]. What happened last time?" |
| "Can you do it cheaper than [freelancer]?" | "Probably not cheaper, but faster and with a guarantee: live in 7 days or 20% back. Which matters more for you: lowest price or a sure result?" |
| "Not now, maybe next month." | "No problem. Want me to hold a slot for [date] and check in on [date]?" (Then move them to Nurture with a date.) |
