## 1. The math: what 25 clients takes

Work backwards from the goal. At realistic conversion rates:

**25 clients ← ~60 proposals ← ~110 discovery calls ← ~350 replies ← ~4,000 first touches**

That works out to **about 9 calls a week** for 13 weeks. It's a volume problem, not a talent problem.

## 4. The manual workflow: our operating system

### 4.1 Pipeline stages (columns in the tracker)

`New → Contacted → Replied → Call Booked → Call Held → Proposal Sent → Won (deposit paid) → Delivered → Referral asked`
Side stages: `Lost (reason)` and `Nurture (follow up in 30/60 days)`.

### 4.2 The sales process: same steps, every lead

1. **Reply fast.** Answer every reply within 1 hour during working hours, and offer two specific call times plus the Calendly link.
2. **Discovery call (20–30 min).** Use the script in `OUTREACH-SCRIPTS.md`. **Always ask for a decision date.**
3. **Proposal within 24 hours.** One page, 3 options (Good / Better / Best), valid for 7 days, payment details included.
4. **Follow up on the proposal** on day 2, day 5 and day 10, then move the lead to Nurture.
5. **Close.** Send the deposit invoice, then the onboarding form, then kick off within 24 hours of payment.
6. **Deliver, then ask.** At handoff, ask for a Google review, a video testimonial and 2 referrals. Write the case study the same week.

### 4.3 Follow-up cadence for cold outreach

**Most deals come from touches 2–5. Never stop at one message.**

| Day | Touch |
|---|---|
| 0 | First message, personalised and short, about their problem |
| 2–3 | Bump: "Did you get a chance to see this?" plus one extra detail |
| 6–7 | Value: send the video audit, mockup or case study |
| 12–14 | Breakup: "Should I close your file?" (this gets a surprising number of replies) |
| +30 | Nurture: something new (a seasonal offer or a fresh result) |

### 4.4 Daily rhythm (Pakistan time)

UAE is 1 hour behind PKT, the UK 5 hours behind and US Eastern 10 hours behind. US 9am is 7pm PKT.

| Time (PKT) | Sales owner | Researcher (part-time) | Delivery lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10:00–10:30 | **Replies first**, update tracker | — | Client delivery |
| 10:30–12:30 | Local calls and WhatsApp (PK/UAE) | Build lists: 40 new leads/day with problem spotted | Delivery |
| 12:30–14:00 | First-touch outreach (email, LinkedIn) | Draft personalisation lines | **1 hr: 2–3 demo mockups** |
| 14:00–15:00 | Break / Zuhr | — | — |
| 15:00–17:30 | Follow-ups, proposals, UK calls | List QA, Fiverr/Upwork inbox | Delivery |
| 18:30–21:30 (3 evenings/week) | **US calls + fresh Upwork jobs** (be in the first 5 proposals) + LinkedIn DMs to US | — | — |

- **Friday:** short day for Jummah. Do content batching and the list top-up.
- **Monday, 30 min, whole team:** scorecard review (§6) and decide what to change this week.

## 7. Closing and pricing rules

- **Cut scope, not price.** If they say it's too expensive, offer a smaller package.
- **Anchor with 3 options.** Most people pick the middle one, so make the middle one the one you want to sell.
- **Every call ends with a next step and a date.** "When will you decide?" "Who else needs to see this?"
- **The proposal expires in 7 days.** Follow up on day 2, day 5 and day 10.
- **Say no to scope creep.** A change request is a new mini-quote.
- **Upsell at delivery:** Launch Site → Lead Engine → Retainer. The best time to sell is when they're happy.

## 8. Delivery: don't let sales outrun delivery

At 25 clients in 13 weeks, delivery becomes the bottleneck by around week 6.

- **Templates first.** Every Launch Site starts from one of the 3 niche templates.
- **Onboarding form** collects logo, photos, services, testimonials and domain access in one go, so content doesn't delay you.
- **Two revision rounds**, written into the proposal.
- **Delivery checklist** before go-live: mobile, speed, forms, WhatsApp button, GA4, Google Business Profile link, SSL, backup.
- **Freelancer bench.** Line up 2–3 vetted freelance developers or designers in Lahore at fixed per-project rates by week 5, before you need them.
- **Capacity rule.** One developer can handle about 3 Launch Sites a week. If signed work exceeds 2 weeks of capacity, bring in the bench.

## 10. Automate later: the automation backlog

Automate a step only after doing it manually at least 20 times and writing it down as an SOP. Priority order:

## 11. Rules and risks

- **Volume and follow-up beat perfection.** The plan fails if outreach drops below target for 2 weeks in a row.
- **Protect the accounts.** Keep WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email sending at human volume and personalised. Spammy blasts get numbers and profiles banned.
- **Stay compliant.** Cold email needs a real sender name, the business address and a one-line opt-out. Email business addresses only, and stop when asked. Don't use autodialers or pre-recorded calls for US numbers, and honour "don't call me again."
- **Don't chase big custom apps first.** Small fast wins build proof and cash. Big projects come from happy small clients.
- **Don't discount to win.** Shrink scope instead.
- **Stay honest.** Real case studies, real numbers, real scarcity only.
