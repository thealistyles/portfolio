# Miyaar reels: competitors and what the algorithm rewards

## What Miyaar sells (from miyaardevelopers.com)

Grey structure and turnkey house construction on the client's own plot in DHA Lahore, Bahria Town, Bahria Orchard, Lake City and Fazaia Housing, from 5 marla to 2 kanal.

**Prices, per sq ft:**

| Work | Price |
|---|---|
| Grey structure | from PKR 3,200 |
| Grey structure with basement | PKR 4,500–5,500 |
| Turnkey | from PKR 7,500 |
| Finishing | from PKR 3,500 |
| Furnishing | from PKR 1,000 |

**Promises:**
- An itemised BOQ that becomes a fixed-price contract.
- Grey structure in 4–5 months and turnkey in 8–10, with the handover date in writing.
- Grade-60 steel and named cement.
- Weekly site video on WhatsApp.
- DHA, LDA, BTAP and Lake City approvals handled.
- Clients in Dubai, London, Toronto, Doha and New York.

**Track record:** 130 projects delivered, building since 2011.

## What the algorithm rewards, and where each reel uses it

| Signal | How to earn it | Reels |
|---|---|---|
| Watch time and completion | A transformation the viewer waits to see finish; awe and surprise drive sharing | 1 (plot to keys), 11, 14 |
| Saves | Practical value: rates, material lists, checklists | 3, 13, 15 |
| Comments | A question or a mild controversy | 2 (lowest quote), 5 (pick 1, 2 or 3) |
| Shares (DM sends) | Something to send to family or a partner, especially overseas | 4, 13, 15 |
| Hook in 0–3 s | Big on-screen text plus the strongest frame | All |
| Mute viewing | All the key words on screen; the voiceover adds to them | All |

Sources: the marketingskills `social` and `video` skills (short-form hooks, B-roll pacing, captions, posting) and the virality research behind them (emotion and practical value as drivers).

## Posting routine

- **Volume:** one reel a day for the first two weeks, plus a post every other day.
- **Sound:** add a trending sound at low volume under the voiceover inside the app.
- **Measure:** log views at 48 hours, watch time, saves, shares and WhatsApp enquiries for each reel. After 10 reels, make more of the top format.
- **Reply fast:** answer every "price?" comment within the hour and move the chat to WhatsApp.
