# Agency Closer (Miyaar Developers)

The path is: reply → 15-minute demo → paid pilot → monthly retainer → referral. Speed wins: answer every reply within 1 hour and book the demo within 48. For deeper offer design (guarantees, bonuses, honest urgency) use the `offers` skill.

## 1. When they reply

- Interested: "Great. I'll show you your own assistant answering your customers. 15 minutes today at [time] or tomorrow at [time]?"
- Asks the price first: give the range and the pilot ("setup from PKR 60k; you can start with a 14-day pilot for PKR 25k, credited if you continue"), then ask for the demo.
- Ask the developer to build their demo agent before the call (the `ai-automation-architect` skill, Step 3).

## 2. The 15-minute demo call

1. **Their evidence (2 min):** "On Tuesday at 8:12 pm I asked about 5 marla plots; the reply came 15 hours later."
2. **Discovery (4 min):** leads per day, who replies now, nights and weekends, value of one customer.
3. **Live demo (5 min):** they message the demo number as a customer, in Roman Urdu and with a tricky question. It answers in seconds, qualifies the lead and books a visit, and the owner gets the alert.
4. **ROI (2 min):** "If you get 40 enquiries a day and lose even 5 to slow replies, at [close rate] and [value], that's PKR [X] a month."
5. **Close (2 min):** "Shall we start the 14-day pilot? PKR 25k now, credited to setup if you continue. We connect your number this week." Send the payment details in the chat while you're still on the call.

## 3. Same-day WhatsApp quote (if they need to think)

> [Name], as discussed: AI WhatsApp assistant for [Business]. It answers every enquiry within 10 seconds, 24/7; asks [budget / area / timeline]; books [site visits / consultations]; alerts you on hot leads; and sends a daily summary. 14-day pilot: PKR [25k], credited to setup (PKR [X]) if you continue; then PKR [Y]/month. Live within 5 days of payment. Payment: [details]. Send the screenshot and we start today.

## 4. Follow-ups

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Day 2 | "Any questions? I can also show the demo to your partner or sales manager." |
| Day 4 | Send a 30-second screen recording of the demo answering a late-night question. |
| Day 7 | "Should I keep your pilot slot for this week, or close the file for now?" |

## 5. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive." | "A night-shift CSR costs PKR 40–60k a month and still sleeps. Start with the 14-day pilot; if it doesn't book visits, you've spent PKR 25k to find out." |
| "AI will say wrong things." | "It only answers from your price sheet, hands complaints and any request for a person to your team, and you approve the script. You can pause it with one word." |
| "Customers want a human." | "They want a fast answer. It handles the first minute and nights; hot leads go straight to your team with their details." |
| "We already reply fast." | Show the timestamps. "This was Tuesday 8:12 pm. Maybe it was a busy night; the assistant covers exactly those nights." |
| "We'll do it next month." | "Fine. Want me to set up the demo on your real price list so it's ready? I'll check in on [date]." |

## 6. After they pay

- Kick off within 24 hours. Collect the facts sheet, prices, FAQs and number access in one form.
- Day 7 and day 14: send the numbers (enquiries answered, average reply time, visits booked).
- At day 14, convert to monthly and ask: "Who are 2 business owners who'd want this?" Referral reward: 10% of setup, or one month free.
- With permission, turn the result into a case study ("reply time from 15 hours to 9 seconds; 23 visits booked in 14 days") and use it in every new pitch.

## Pricing guardrails

- Cut scope, not price; the pilot is the discount.
- Take the first 2 clients at pilot price in exchange for a case study and a video testimonial.
- No work starts without payment.
